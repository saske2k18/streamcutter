StreamCutter

A personal desktop tool that turns my own livestream recordings into short vertical clips with captions.

What it does

  • Transcribes a stream recording locally on my own computer.
  • Picks interesting moments with a local language model.
  • Renders 9:16 clips with word-by-word captions.
  • Uploads the finished clips to my own YouTube channel and TikTok account, only after I sign in and authorize it.

Who it is for

StreamCutter is a single-user tool. It runs only on its owner's computer, is not offered as a service, and has no other users.
